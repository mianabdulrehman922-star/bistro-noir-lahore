Located on MM Alam Road in Gulberg III, Bistro Noir Lahore introduces an elegant dining atmosphere pairing modern European bistro influences with signature flame-grilled steaks, rich hand-crafted pasta, and artisanal wood-fired pizzas.

With deep noir interiors, soft golden lighting, and meticulous plating, Bistro Noir offers a serene environment for intimate family dinners, business lunches, or relaxed weekend coffee gatherings.
